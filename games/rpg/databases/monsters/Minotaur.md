### Minotaur

  ----------------- -------------------------------------------
Armor Class:      14 (12)
Hit Dice:         6
No. of Attacks:   1 gore, 1 bite or 1 weapon
Damage:           1d6 gore, 1d6 bite, 1d6+2 or by weapon +2
Movement:         30' Unarmored 40'
No. Appearing:    1d6, Wild 1d8, Lair 1d8
Save As:          Fighter: 6
Morale:           11
Treasure Type:    C
XP:               500
  ----------------- -------------------------------------------

Minotaurs are huge bull-headed humanoid monsters,
standing more than 7 feet tall and weighing nearly 500 pounds. Most
minotaurs are very aggressive, and fly into a murderous rage if provoked
or hungry. Although minotaurs are not especially intelligent, they
possess innate cunning and logical ability. They never become lost (even
in mazes), and can track enemies with 85% accuracy. They gain +2 to
damage when using melee weapons due to their great Strength. Minotaurs
often wear toughened hides for armor.
