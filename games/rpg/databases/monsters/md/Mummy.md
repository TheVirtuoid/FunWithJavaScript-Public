### Mummy

  ----------------- --------------------
Armor Class:      17 (m) (see below)
Hit Dice:         5\*\*
No. of Attacks:   1 touch
Damage:           1d12 + disease
Movement:         20'
No. Appearing:    1d4, Lair 1d12
Save As:          Fighter: 5
Morale:           12
Treasure Type:    D
XP:               450
  ----------------- --------------------

Mummies are **undead** monsters, magically animated corpses wrapped in
burial cloths. They are normally man-sized, but due to their desiccation
one will normally not weigh more than about 100 pounds.

As they are undead, mummies are immune to **sleep**,
**charm**, and **hold** magic. They can only be injured by spells, fire,
or magical weapons; furthermore, magic weapons do only half damage,
while any sort of fire-based attack does double damage. Those injured by
mummy attacks will contract **mummy rot**, a disease that prevents
normal or magical healing; a **cure disease** spell must be applied to
the victim before they may again regain hit points.
