### Sea Serpent

  ----------------- ------------------
Armor Class:      17
Hit Dice:         6
No. of Attacks:   1 bite
Damage:           2d6
Movement:         Swim 50' (10')
No. Appearing:    Wild 2d6
Save As:          Fighter: 6
Morale:           8
Treasure Type:    None
XP:               500
  ----------------- ------------------

Sea serpents are, obviously, serpentine monsters which
live in the sea. They range from 20' to 40' long. A sea serpent can
choose to wrap around a ship and constrict; in this case, roll 2d10 for
damage to the vehicle, and reduce any effective Hardness by half..
