### Rock Baboon

  ----------------- --------------------------------
Armor Class:      14
Hit Dice:         2
No. of Attacks:   1 club or 1 fist, 1 bite
Damage:           1d6 club or 1d4 fist, 1d4 bite
Movement:         40'
No. Appearing:    2d6, Wild 2d6, Lair 5d6
Save As:          Fighter: 2
Morale:           8
Treasure Type:    None
XP:               75
  ----------------- --------------------------------

Rock baboons are a large, particularly intelligent variety of baboon. An
adult male rock baboon is 4' to 5' tall and weighs 200 to 250 pounds,
with females being a bit smaller and lighter.

Rock baboons are omnivorous, but prefer meat. They are
aggressive, naturally cruel creatures. They will prepare ambushes in
rocky or forested terrain and attack any party they outnumber.
