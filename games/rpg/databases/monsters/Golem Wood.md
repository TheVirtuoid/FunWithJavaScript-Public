### Golem, Wood

  ----------------- ------------
Armor Class:      13 (m)
Hit Dice:         2+2\*
No. of Attacks:   1 fist
Damage:           1d8 fist
Movement:         40'
No. Appearing:    1
Save As:          Fighter: 1
Morale:           12
Treasure Type:    None
XP:               100
  ----------------- ------------

Wood golems are small constructs, not more than 4' in
height, and are crudely made. Being made of wood makes them vulnerable
to fire-based attacks; thus, wood golems suffer one extra point of
damage per die from fire; any saving throws against such effects are at
a penalty of -2. They move stiffly, suffering a -1 penalty to
Initiative.
