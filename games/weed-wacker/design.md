# Weed Wacker

## Summary
A game where the player uses a weed wacker to cut down weeds. Points are gained for each week cut down, and lost for hitting rocks and other obstables. Player can use points to upgrade their wacker.

## The Wacker

### Properties
1. Power - The strength to cut down weeds. Weeds differ in size and toughness.
2. Speed - The speed at which the wacker can move.
3. Range - The distance the wacker can reach to cut down weeds.
4. Durability - The amount of damage the wacker can withstand before breaking.

## The Weeds
Various weeds take more power to cut down, but will give more points

### Properties
1. Toughness - The toughness of the weed, which affects the power required to cut it down.
2. Points - the number of points the weed is worth.
3. Type - the type of the weed

### Different Weeds
1. There will be 10 different weeds, each with a toughness rating and a point value
2. OPTIONAL: There will be 3 different Boss Weeds, that can do damage as you try to cut them down.


## The Rocks
1. There are 5 different rocks, each with a toughness rating. They cannot be cut down.
2. They never disappear from the screen.
3. When they are hit, they deal damage to the player. Every second they are being hit, they deal damage.


## Upgrades
1. Power - Increase the power of the wacker.
2. Speed - Increase the speed of the wacker.
3. Range - Increase the range of the wacker.
4. Durability - Increase the durability of the wacker.
5. Time - Increases the time the player has to clear a level.
6. Spawn - Increase the rate at which weeds spawn.


## Play
1. The player has 10 seconds to clear a level. They will never "clear" a level, only play on that level for that time.
2. Once the time is up, the player goes to an upgrade screen, where they can use their points to upgrade their wacker.
3. If their durability is depleted during game play, they lose the game.
